# **Seven Families Card Game - App Overview**

The **Seven Families** app is a digital version of the classic card game **Seven Families**, designed for **2 to 6 players**. This app allows players to experience the traditional game in a fun and interactive way, using a custom-designed deck of **42 cards** divided into **7 families**. The goal is to collect all six cards from a single family to complete it and win the game.

---

## **Objective of the Game**
The main objective of the game is to **collect all six members** of a family and form complete families. The player with the **most completed families** at the end of the game wins.

---

## **Features of the App**
1. **Interactive Gameplay**:  
   - Players can ask for specific cards from other players to complete their families.
   - If a player doesn’t have the requested card, the game simulates the "Go fish!" rule by drawing a card from the central deck.

2. **Card Management**:  
   - The app automatically manages the cards in your hand and the central deck, simplifying the process of asking for and exchanging cards.
  
3. **Random Card Selection**:  
   - Players receive a random family card when they click to reveal a bonus, with animations showing the flip of the card.
  
4. **Animations and Effects**:  
   - The card flipping animation adds a dynamic element to the game, making the experience more immersive.

---

## **How to Play the Game**

1. **Setup**:  
   - The deck of 42 cards is shuffled and dealt evenly among the players.
   - The remaining cards form the draw pile in the center of the screen.

2. **Gameplay**:
   - Players take turns in a clockwise order.
   - On a player’s turn, they ask another player for a specific card. For example, "Do you have the father from the Smith family?"
   - The player must already have at least one card from the family they are asking about.
   - If the asked player has the requested card, they must give it to the asking player. The asking player then continues their turn.
   - If the asked player does not have the card, they respond with "Go fish!" The asking player then draws a card from the central pile, ending their turn.

3. **Completing a Family**:  
   - A family is complete when a player has all six members of that family. When a player completes a family, it is displayed face-up on the screen.

4. **Ending the Game**:  
   - The game ends when all families have been completed or when no more moves can be made.
   - The player with the most complete families at the end of the game is the winner.

---

## **How the App Works**

1. **User Interface**:  
   - The game displays your cards, the available families, and the game’s progress.
   - The card flip effect visually simulates turning over cards, adding excitement to the gameplay.
  
2. **Bonus Cards**:  
   - When the "Reveal Bonus Card" button is clicked, the game flips the card to display a random bonus from a list, offering players extra chances to interact and get unique bonuses.

3. **Card Collection**:  
   - The app keeps track of your progress in collecting all members of each family, and you can see which families are completed.

---

## **Strategies to Win**
1. **Memory**:  
   - Pay close attention to the cards requested by other players, as this will help you deduce which cards they need and which ones they have.
   
2. **Focus on One Family**:  
   - Rather than trying to complete several families at once, focus on completing one family at a time for a higher chance of success.

3. **Efficient Card Requests**:  
   - Ask for cards that you know your opponents are likely to have, based on their previous actions and requests.

4. **Plan Ahead**:  
   - Keep track of the cards in the central pile and anticipate what others might need, allowing you to control the flow of the game.
